Ridesharing Is Broken for Riders and Drivers

Traditional ridesharing oligopolies trap users in predatory cycles of surging prices and broken UX.

🔀
App Fragmentation
Riders manually toggle between Uber, Lyft, and Waymo to compare prices and ETAs, wasting minutes and cognitive energy before every trip.

Predatory Surge Pricing
Algorithms spike prices up to 300% during rain, events, or commute hours with zero transparency or alternative route discovery.

Cancellation Hell
Drivers cancel after 10 minutes of waiting. Riders are forced to restart the search, suffer higher surge fares, and endure double card holds.

The Solution 03 / 10

Easy Ride: Autonomous AI Rideshare Dispatch

An intelligent agent that negotiates, locks funds in smart escrow, and auto-recovers — entirely in 1-second USDC.

🤖
Conversational AI Dispatch
Simply prompt: "Get me to the airport under $35, EV preferred." Gemini AI translates natural language into structured multi-provider bids.
🛡️
Smart Contract Escrow
Fares are securely held in EasyRideEscrow.sol on Arc Testnet. Funds are never released until the ride safely completes — zero fraud.
🔄
Autonomous Recovery
If a driver cancels, the agent reassigns to the next best ride instantly while keeping escrowed funds locked. No double billing, ever.
✨ The Result: Zero comparison fatigue, guaranteed transparent fares, and instantaneous 1-second on-chain settlement.
